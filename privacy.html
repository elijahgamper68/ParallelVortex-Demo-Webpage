Privacy notice

Last updated November 4, 2026

Parallel Vortex is a small tech consulting and web design business based in Ottawa, Ontario. This notice explains, in plain language, what information this website collects, why, and what we do with it. We follow Canada's private-sector privacy law (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • When you send a message: your name, email address and whatever you write in the message.
  • When you book a consultation: your name, email address, the time you choose and anything you tell us about what you need help with.

This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies, and we don't sell or rent anyone's information.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and arrange your consultation.
  • To send the Google Meet link and any follow-up you've asked for.
  • To provide the services you ask for and keep the business and tax records we're required to keep.

Services that help run this site

A few trusted services handle parts of this site for us, and only receive what they need to do their job: our scheduling tool (for bookings and Google Meet links), our form service (to deliver contact messages to our inbox), our website host, and Google Fonts (which loads the site's typeface). Each has its own privacy policy. Some of these services may store information outside Canada.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to help you, and for as long as business and tax rules require. After that we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (unless we're required to keep it). Just send a message through the contact form and we'll take care of it.

Questions

If you have any questions about this notice or how your information is handled, please get in touch through our contact form.
